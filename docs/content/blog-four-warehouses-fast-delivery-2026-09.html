Updated September 2026. If you have read older reviews mentioning slow delivery, this post explains what has changed. SIHOO Australia now holds stock in four warehouses – Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane and Perth – so orders are dispatched the next business day from the warehouse nearest you and usually arrive within one to three business days.

Why four warehouses matter

Australia is a big country, and an ergonomic chair is a big box. When every order shipped from one location, a customer in Perth could wait more than a week while the carton crossed the Nullarbor. Holding stock in each major region means your chair is usually already in your state when you order it.

  • Sydney – serves NSW and the ACT
  • Melbourne – serves Victoria and Tasmania
  • Brisbane – serves Queensland and northern NSW
  • Perth – serves Western Australia

South Australia and the Northern Territory are served from the nearest warehouse with stock, which usually means Melbourne or Brisbane.

How long delivery takes

DestinationTypical delivery after dispatch
Metro Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth1–3 business days
Other capital cities and large regional centres2–5 business days
Regional and remote addresses5–10 business days

These are courier transit times once your order has been dispatched. Every order is dispatched the next business day. You receive a tracking link by email and SMS as soon as the courier scans the parcel.

What if my chair is out of stock locally?

If the colour or model you ordered is not in your nearest warehouse, we ship it from the next closest one and tell you on the product page before you order. Products with a longer lead time show a High demand notice with the extra days added, so there are no surprises after checkout.

Free delivery, Australia-wide

Delivery is free on every chair to every Australian address, including regional and remote postcodes. There is no minimum order and no surcharge for larger items.

Damaged carton or missing parts?

Chairs are packed in double-wall cartons with foam inserts, but couriers are not gentle. If your carton arrives damaged, photograph it before opening and contact us at support@sihoo.com.au or 1300 002 580. We send replacement parts from the nearest warehouse, usually within two business days, under the three-year Australian warranty.

Where we are

SIHOO Australia is an Australian company based in South Granville, Sydney, with our own local support team. Every chair we sell is covered by Australian Consumer Law and a three-year warranty serviced from Australia, not overseas.
